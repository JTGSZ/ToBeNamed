-Channels
Byond allows your client to have 1024 sound channels
Sounds default to either queueing or overwriting each other if in the same channel
By default it will try to dole all them retards out on its own
Which includes the sounds doling themselves out ontop of each other and forcing their predecessors silent

Also the amount of sounds played at once without the client mangling them to sound like ass is way less than 1024

-Sound Environments
Setting a sound environment on any sound will make all sounds played to the client also share that environment
Echo can be set per individual sound and also interacts with the environment settings apparently.
So with this you could set environment for all sounds via any sound, and then use echo to remove chunks of it from any individual sound.

-516 3D audio helpers (Atom) (3d Sound Source helper)
Currently in 516 sound gained a new variable called atom which replaces needing to run a continuous loop for sfx apparently
Problems with it I have found are the following.
When the sound source (atom) moves it doesn't update their position relative to the client receiving it
When the client receiving it loses sight of the sound source, it currently turns it off
When they see it again it turns on
If you have been sent a sound with atom linked to a sound source the moment you see where they have emitted it starts playing even if you were offscreen initially


Supported file formats:
MIDI - (.mid or .midi), 
module formats - .mod, .it, .s3m, .xm, and .oxm  (These are pretty fun, theres even a big azz archive website for them https://modarchive.org/)
Regular formats - .wav, .ogg, .mp3, .raw, .wma, and .aiff
